The stacks, verbatim

--font (body voice)
"Archivo", "Helvetica Neue", -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif

Record names, prose, values. Bold + sentence-case for names — never caps.

--font-mono (stamped voice)
ui-monospace, "Cascadia Code", "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace

Labels, chips (Signal/Gate/Stamp), IDs, and every tabular number/$ figure. Uppercase, ~0.03–0.06em tracking, 700–800 weight.

Changed 2026-07-25 — the body voice is Archivo. The kit shipped on "Geist" (the then-current style.css token); the atom consistency pass replaced it with "Archivo". Archivo is a grotesque with a taller x-height and squarer terminals — it holds up better at 12–13px on the dark panels, and its condensed cousins sit closer to the stamped mono voice than Geist's humanist curves did. Fonts are CDN-loaded, not bundled, so shipping this is a one-line change to the Google Fonts <link> in index.html. --font-mono is untouched.
