9-slice vs. scaling the same asset

Identical export in both rows. The slice holds the chamfer angle, the rail notches and the 3px scanline at every width; scaling rakes the chamfer and smears the notches. This is the whole argument for the method.

Why round on the vertical axis. The growable band is rows 43–81 — 39 rows, exactly 13 periods of the 3px scanline, so it is phase-closed and tiles seamlessly. stretch would drift the period to 9px at h=300 and 25px at h=700 and destroy the texture. Horizontally there is no countable rhythm, so the edges stretch.

Text is not artwork. The Figma node bakes "FAILED" dead centre in the stretch zone, where no slice numbers can save it. The export hides that TEXT node and the DOM supplies the words instead — which is also what lets one plate serve every group.
