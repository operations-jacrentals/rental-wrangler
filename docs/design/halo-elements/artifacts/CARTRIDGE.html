--f1/--f2 are deliberately left off this rail. The part's own header lists them as safe knobs for the top chamfer's fade stops, but they're declared and consumed entirely inside .pf-fct's own rule, not on the root — a single root-level data-knobbed target can't reach them; the value would read empty and writing it would do nothing. Move them by hand-editing .pf-fct in parts/cartridge.css directly. (.pf-fcb declares --f1/--f2 too, but re-declares its whole background with the numbers inlined, so on the bottom facet they're already inert in the source — untouched here either way.)

Both stages are held at 280px tall on purpose, not for symmetry: below roughly 256px Chromium takes a different raster path and shifts the .pf-rface chrome-edge gradient one pixel left of the true render — documented on the part's own preview page. The extra top/bottom air also covers the real overflow: painted height is 127, not the nominal 125 (.pf-blt/.pf-blb escape .pf-body's clip), and .pf-brk's blur bleeds ~1px past its own rects.
