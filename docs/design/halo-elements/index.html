c9 · circuit field.halo-etch
PASS · see note

The etched copper field under the header row: 4 stacked background layers, an inline SVG of traces and pads, a block of micro technical text. Only band-B-only part with two real pixel-gate windows.

Not independent. 3 of its 4 background layers replay light baked in from other parts (--layer1 = c4's rim spill, --layer2 = c3's red wash, --layer3 = c11's shadow). Move those parts and c9's baked light goes stale. The .is-unlit variant (not in the mockup) turns them off.
Two box-sized windows gate 100% of the pixels that are c9 alone: primary 139×135@(11,241) → PASS mean 0.475, max 3. supplementary 336×40@(11,336) → FAIL mean 1.111, max 2 (0.111 over the bar) — re-confirmed independently. This is a Chromium screen-space dither re-phasing when the raster viewport shrinks to 40px tall, not a value error: rendered at its true position inside the full-card assembly (a real 1292×635 stage), the same pixels score mean 0.000 — re-confirmed independently above. Both windows are documented in etch.verify-right.html.
